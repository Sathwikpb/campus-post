CAMPUS POST · INDEPENDENT STUDENT PROJECT

Privacy policy

Applies to Campus Post 0.3.5. Prepared 24 October 2026.

Campus Post is an independent browser extension for TU Ilmenau webmail. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by TU Ilmenau or Google. Published by the Campus Post independent student project. This policy describes extension version 0.3.5, distributed as an early-access build. It has not been published in the Chrome Web Store.

What the extension accesses

After you explicitly enable email tools, Campus Post accesses your TU account identifier, mailbox message identifiers, sender names and addresses, subjects, dates, email text and links. Scanning retrieves email source through your existing signed-in TU webmail session. That source may contain MIME attachment data; messages above 6 MB are skipped. It does not open or translate attachments. Page translation reads visible non-editable text in TU webmail and same-origin frames.

It uses the webmail request token in memory and the browser’s existing authenticated session for requests to TU. It does not read password fields, extract cookie values, or send authentication information to the developer. TU continues to receive ordinary webmail requests.

Purpose and processing

Access is used only to display inbox digests, identify useful links and dates, translate text, and prepare an agenda or calendar draft. Ordinary key-sentence and date extraction uses local rules. Optional browser models can produce translations and summaries on supported devices, after a model download. Email content used by these browser APIs stays on the device. Downloads and browser services are subject to your browser provider’s policies.

The extension has no developer backend, advertising, analytics, telemetry, or sale of data. The supplied code does not send mailbox content to its developer.

Local storage and retention

Recent decoded email bodies and digest metadata are held in Chrome extension session storage, which is temporary browser-session memory. This keeps the current tab’s recent messages available when webmail navigates or reloads. Each tab caches up to 500 messages and approximately 3 MB, with a global cache budget that can evict older tabs. Large messages may not fit. Raw MIME source is not cached. The browser may discard session data on restart, extension disable, reload or update.

The mailbox tab’s cache is removed when that tab closes. Settings → Stop access and erase all local data clears the session cache as well as consent, agendas and preferences. The × button only hides the popup. Each cached tab is bound to the verified account hash; a different account cannot restore it. No email archive is stored in extension disk storage.

Preferences, consent and agenda records persist in extension local storage in this browser profile. Agenda records can contain event titles, locations, source excerpts and links. Account hashing separates accounts; it is not encryption. Calendar downloads remain where you save them.

When information leaves your device

  • Google Translate: only clicking “Send this part to Google Translate” opens Google’s site with that selected email part and language. Long emails are split into parts. The text appears in the destination URL and can be retained in browser history.
  • Google Calendar: clicking “Add to Google Calendar” opens Google’s site with the event title, dates, location, description and relevant links. It creates a draft; you save it at Google. Its URL can be retained in browser history.
  • Other email links: clicking registration, meeting, reply or other links opens their destinations, which receive normal browser requests. Their own privacy policies apply.

Use of those services is optional. Campus Post does not create a Google account connection or automatically sync with Google Calendar. Read Google’s privacy policy for Google’s handling.

Your controls and deletion

You may decline initial consent and the extension will not access email data. You can disable periodic checking in Inbox, clear the current account’s saved agenda in Settings, or choose “Stop access and erase all local data” to revoke consent and erase all extension local storage. Close every other Campus Post panel and reload webmail afterward to remove their page-memory copies. The erase control also clears the browser-session email cache. Uninstalling also removes extension storage. These actions do not delete downloads, browser history or events already saved in Google Calendar.

Limited use and updates

Campus Post’s use and transfer of user data adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Information is used to provide the prominently described email tools, not for advertising or unrelated purposes. Changes to data handling require an updated policy and, where necessary, renewed consent.

Support

See the support guide. The public support guide is available at Campus Post Help. Individual support is not available during early access. The help website is hosted through OpenAI Sites; visits are handled by the hosting provider. The website has no mailbox connection, analytics script or email upload form. An individual publisher contact will be provided in the Chrome Web Store listing if the project is submitted there.
