CAMPUS POST · YOUR CHOICE

Enable your email tools?

Campus Post is an independent student project, not an official TU Ilmenau service.

After you agree, it uses your existing signed-in TU webmail session to read your account identifier, senders, subjects, message contents, links and dates. It can scan the inbox and check again every five minutes while open. Your password stays with TU.

Email analysis runs in this browser. Recent decoded emails stay in temporary browser-session memory so opening another message does not erase the digest. They are cleared when the mailbox tab closes, the browser session ends, or you erase extension data (× only hides the popup); saved agenda entries (which can contain email excerpts and links) and preferences stay in this browser profile.

Optional browser translation and summaries require supported Chrome models and downloads. Ordinary key-sentence extraction works without AI. Opening Google Translate sends the selected email part to Google; Add to Google Calendar sends the event details to Google only when you click it.

No developer server, analytics or advertising receives your mailbox. You can stop checking in Settings, clear saved data, or remove the extension.

Add to Google Calendar

Choose an event’s “Add to Google Calendar” button. It opens a prepared event in Google Calendar with its dates, location and relevant links. Check it and press Save there. No developer setup, API key, or paid integration is needed.

READ IN YOUR LANGUAGE

Translate the whole webmail page.

Translate visible text in the connected TU webmail tab, including same-origin email previews. Original text is kept until you restore it or reload the page.

Chrome downloads language packs when needed. Images, attachments, PDFs, hidden content, cross-origin frames and editable fields are not translated. Click again after opening a new email.

Browser translation fallback

If this computer has no built-in Translator API, open webmail, right-click the page and choose Translate, then select your language. The extension cannot activate Chrome’s translation menu itself. Google Translate email links are also available in the reader.

Set up your reading language

Uses browser-managed on-device translation where available. Once a language pack is installed, opening another German email translates it automatically. This never sends email to Google Translate automatically.

Choose the email’s original language. For bilingual messages, read the existing English version if available. Changing the target language does not translate Campus Post’s interface.

Browser summaries are optional. Email reading works without a model.

Built-in summaries use Chrome’s on-device AI, with a model download and hardware requirements. They need no AI account or subscription. Key-sentence summaries remain available everywhere; they do not provide semantic understanding.

What runs automatically

  • Inbox checking runs every five minutes only while this dashboard and a signed-in TU tab stay open. Background-tab throttling can delay it.
  • Automatic local agenda entries include explicit future dates without booking, cancellation, recurrence or time-zone warnings.
  • Google Calendar opens only when you choose “Add to Google Calendar”. You check and save the event there.

Privacy

Recent decoded emails remain in temporary browser-session memory across webmail reloads, limited to 500 messages and about 3 MB per tab. Closing the mailbox tab, ending the browser session or erasing extension data clears this cache. The × button only hides the popup. Your local agenda and preferences are saved in this browser profile, separately for each TU account. Google Translate receives only the email part you choose to open there. Local translation and summaries do not upload email content. Choosing Add to Google Calendar sends that event’s title, dates, location and description to Google.

Clearing the local agenda does not delete events you saved in Google Calendar.
